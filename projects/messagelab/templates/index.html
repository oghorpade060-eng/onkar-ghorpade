The idea in one minute

TextTF–IDF featuresClassifierLabel

Classification chooses a category. TF–IDF converts words into numerical features. Precision asks how often a spam prediction is right; recall asks how much actual spam is caught.

Historical English SMS data cannot establish performance on today's scams, email or other languages. A ham result is not a safety guarantee.
