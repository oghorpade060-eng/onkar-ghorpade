# Learning and experiment log

Use this after doing the work. Do not mark a topic complete just because its code is available.

| Date | Topic or experiment | What I ran or changed | Result or error | What I can explain | Next step |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

## Weekly review

- What can I now build without a full solution in front of me?
- Which assumption did I test?
- What failed, and how did I investigate it?
- What is one limitation I can explain honestly?
- What is the smallest useful task for next week?

For ML experiments, record the data version, split, seed, candidate configuration and validation result. Keep final test data out of repeated design decisions. For SQL, record which rows your filters include or exclude and test at least one boundary date.
